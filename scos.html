SonoFront Clinical Operations System (SCOS)

About SCOS

The SonoFront Clinical Operations System (SCOS) is a structured governance and operational coordination framework designed to manage medical device deployment, compliance enforcement, training oversight, insurance alignment, and financial reconciliation.

SCOS operates as a governance layer over device custodianship and institutional coordination. It does not replace institutional EMR systems or independent clinical judgement.

Core Governance Scope

  • Device custodianship oversight
  • Training administration & certification governance
  • Financial reconciliation structures
  • Insurance compliance coordination
  • Operational audit readiness
  • Structured communication channels
  • Analytics & executive reporting

Mission

To operate medical technology as governed infrastructure rather than transactional equipment.

Vision

To build scalable, audit-ready, insurance-aligned clinical operations frameworks across Africa and globally.

Core Principles

  • Governance before convenience
  • Evidence before assumption
  • Automation before manual chasing
  • Compliance before scale
  • Accountability before expansion
